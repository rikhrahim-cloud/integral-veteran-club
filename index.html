About
the club

Integral Veteran Club was founded by a group of players who'd aged out of competitive league football but had no intention of giving it up.

Our story

What started as a handful of neighbours meeting on Saturdays has grown into a properly run club with an executive bureau, a registered membership, and friendly fixtures against veteran sides across Yaoundé.

The club takes its name and crest from the idea of playing the whole game — fitness, brotherhood, and community, not just the ninety minutes. Members join from 25 upward, and everyone gets minutes.

Home matches and Saturday training are played at our pitch in Nomayos.

What we stand for

Everyone plays

Selection balances form with fairness. If you train and your dues are current, you get minutes.

Health first

Veteran football is about staying fit for the long run. Warm-ups are compulsory and nobody plays hurt.

The club is the members

Bureau positions are elected and every active member has a vote at the General Assembly.
